Webapp - Finnish election promises vs actual outcome

Project goal:
Create a web app that tracks Finnish parliamentary election promises and compares them to what happens during the term in office. The app should help users understand whether parties and individual elected candidates fulfilled their promises, and how that relates to broader economic and social conditions.

Core concept:
A candidate usually represents a political party. The party may have shared campaign themes and promises, while candidates also have their own personal promises. This project should track both party-level and candidate-level commitments.

Scope:
- Focus on Finnish parliamentary elections.
- Support multiple election cycles, beginning with the most recent parliamentary elections.
- Show elected candidates, their party membership, and the promises they supported or made themselves.
- Track 0-100 KPI progress of each promise across a four-year period.
- Include economic and social indicators during each election period.

Main features:
1. Election overview
   - list election years and general data
   - show which parties won seats and whether they were in government or opposition
   - display vote share and party priorities during the campaign

2. Party page
   - list all parties in a given election year
   - show vote share, seats, government status, and main topics
   - include a municipality-level vote map idea for future development

3. Candidate page
   - list elected candidates by party
   - show each candidate's main campaign promises
   - connect each promise to legislative actions, discussions, proposals, and policy developments during the term
   - present a KPI score from 0 to 100 for the completion or progress of each promise

4. Economic and social dashboard
   - show unemployment rate, GDP growth, consumer confidence, inflation, and other relevant indicators
   - compare the relevant election period to the party or government in power

5. Future user contribution layer
   - allow users to suggest a campaign promise they believe a candidate or party made
   - include pending review state for moderation and verification
   - store source links and notes together with every suggested promise

Technology plan:
- Frontend: React + Vite
- Backend: Node.js + Express
- Database: PostgreSQL (preferred via Supabase or similar managed Postgres)
- Hosting: Vercel for frontend, Render or similar for backend, Supabase for database

Data sources to use:
- https://www.fsd.tuni.fi/pohtiva/
- https://www.yle.fi
- public election result data and official government/parliamentary records
- policy documents, parliamentary proceedings, and initiative tracking sources

Priority roadmap:
1. Build core election, party, candidate, and promise models
2. Add API endpoints for reading and listing data
3. Create simple dashboard UI for party overview and candidate scorecards
4. Add notes and source links to each promise
5. Add moderation flow for user-submitted promises
6. Add economic indicator comparisons and progress charts
7. Extend with interactive maps and deeper policy tracking

Acceptance criteria:
- The app can display an election year and all main parties.
- The app can show elected candidates and their corresponding promises.
- Progress of each promise can be tracked as a KPI.
- Users can submit potential campaign promises for review.
- The platform stores source material and statuses for each promise.
